- `deepseek.svg`: whale path extracted, unchanged in color and outline, from [DeepSeek’s official logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-LLM/6712a86bfb7dd25c73383c5ad2eb7a8db540258b/images/logo.svg) ([repository](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-LLM)). Only the canvas and wordmark exclusion differ. Original blue: `#4D6BFE`.
- `openrouter.svg`, `openrouter-dark.svg`: original Grape and Cloud glyphs from [OpenRouter Brand Assets](https://openrouter.ai/brand/), downloaded 2026-10-21. [Grape](https://openrouter.ai/brand/logos/transparent/glyph/svg/glyph-grape.svg) · [Cloud](https://openrouter.ai/brand/logos/transparent/glyph/svg/glyph-cloud.svg). Preserve their color, outline and proportions as requested by OpenRouter.
- Apple: the UI uses the macOS system-font `` glyph. No third-party Apple illustration is bundled.
